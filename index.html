A press for parallel texts

Lay a book beside its translations. Upload the same work in two, three or four languages and Pamphleteer sets them in facing columns, chapter by chapter, paragraph by paragraph. Files are read in your browser and never uploaded.

Missing a translation?

Leave a side empty and Pamphleteer will translate the first text into that side’s language. This works for short texts (up to characters); longer works should be uploaded in every language.
